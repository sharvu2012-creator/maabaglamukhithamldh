The Deity

About Maa Baglamukhi

Maa Baglamukhi is worshipped as one of the ten Mahavidyas of the Hindu tantric tradition — forms of the Divine Mother associated with speech, clarity, and steadfastness of mind. Devotees turn to her for stillness amid confusion and strength in the face of adversity.

The Beej Mala observed each Thursday at the Dham is a focused chanting practice offered in her name — a discipline of sound and attention rather than a spectacle. It is kept simple and unhurried, in keeping with the quiet nature of the practice itself.
